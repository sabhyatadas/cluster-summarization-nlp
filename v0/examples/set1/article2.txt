Cluster-based extractive summarization partitions long articles into topical groupings before generating representative summaries for each sub-section. By leveraging dense sentence embeddings, semantic relationships between disparate sentences are captured effectively. Agglomerative hierarchical clustering and k-means clustering represent two prevalent techniques for grouping sentence vectors. The resulting cluster representations are passed to generative transformer decoders, enabling coherent multi-document and long-document synthesis without exceeding architectural positional bounds.
