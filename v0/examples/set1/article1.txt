Natural language processing has undergone significant transformations with the emergence of pretrained transformer architectures. In particular, models trained on large multilingual corpora have demonstrated remarkable transfer learning capabilities across downstream tasks such as document classification, machine translation, and question answering. Sequence-to-sequence summarization models, however, still encounter substantial compute bottlenecks when presented with extremely long texts exceeding context window limits. Extractive methods paired with clustering techniques offer an effective strategy for compressing large documents while maintaining semantic coverage.
